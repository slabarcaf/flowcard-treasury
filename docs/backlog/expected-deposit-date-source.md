# Trusted expected-deposit date source for underwriting

Inflow reliability depends on comparing actual deposits with trusted expected
dates, but the current Salesforce snapshot does not include expected
receivables or deposit dates. Decide the source of record, customer and
counterparty matching key, date semantics, and completeness rules for the
90-day underwriting profile. Do not infer due dates from settlement time or
free-form model output; keep production decisions fail-closed until the source
is mapped.
